# CR-040 Partial payments

Optional, off-by-default. A qualifying percentage of a **service** invoice can activate, restore, or (if allowed) extend that service only, with pro-rata validity rounded down.

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Settings → Partial payments | Network policy. Default off, 50% minimum, 10–90% bounds. Customer and service overrides optional. New-service activation and expired restore on; active extend/renew off; approval off. |
| Customer / service record | Inherit, enable, or disable. Effective line: Partial payment, minimum %, eligible. History and staff approval. |
| Add customer / service | Third activation: Activate after qualifying partial payment. After full payment stays full-only. |
| Payment | Same invoice, ledger, STK, paybill. Eligibility and validity are recalculated on the server. Frontend cannot set the percentage. |
| Validity | `floor(package period × this payment ÷ full amount)`. Cumulative paid decides whether it qualifies. Full remainder tops the same invoice up to one package period. |
| Portal | Amount input, live %, estimated days and expiry (dd/mm/yy). Below-minimum STK is refused. Paybill still accepts and posts without access. |
| SMS | Below minimum, received, activated, restored, remaining balance. Failed SMS does not reverse the payment. |
| Reports | Partial ledger, below-min vs qualifying, activated/restored counts, average validity, CSV export. |

Permissions: `billing.partial.manage` (owner, admin, finance, customer care), `billing.partial.approve` (owner, admin, finance). Technician cannot. RLS on policy and events.

Proof: `partial-payment.test.ts`, `rbac.test.ts`. Live M-Pesa/SMS is not sent in CI.
