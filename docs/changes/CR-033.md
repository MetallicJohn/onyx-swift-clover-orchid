# CR-033 Insights retention KPIs

Tenant Insights (`/app/reports`) now has a **Retention** tab with the suggested retention KPI table:

| KPI | What to measure |
|---|---|
| Monthly churn | % of active customers who leave |
| Renewal rate | % of customers who renew |
| Reactivation rate | % of disconnected customers who return |
| Customer satisfaction | Survey score / CSAT |
| Complaint resolution | Time to resolve and repeat complaints |
| Referral customers | New customers from existing customers |

This-month figures come from the live tenant book (customers, invoices, payments, tickets, referrals, restore notices). CSAT is listed as **Not collected** — no survey score is invented.

See `src/lib/isp/retention.ts`. Proven in `retention.test.ts`.
