# CR-010 billing and invoicing refine

## Customer invoice desk

- Line items from the customer’s packages; optional extra lines
- Optional exclusive Kenya VAT (default off, 16% when enabled)
- Partial payments (`paid_kes`, status `partial`)
- Summary cards, aging strip, status filter, invoice detail + print
- STK amount is the remaining balance

## Platform SaaS billing

- Settings → Plan shows Trial / Starter / Growth with limits and price
- Paid plan changes issue a `SUB-` invoice; the plan activates only after M-Pesa or a recorded receipt
- Trial remains immediate; switching back to trial voids an unpaid platform invoice
- Stripe is still skipped

## Proofs

- `billing.test.ts` — VAT 16% exclusive, remaining/partial, line items
- `saas.test.ts` — starter invoice does not activate until paid; trial voids pending
- `access-policy.test.ts` — partial overdue payment does not restore
