# CR-043 Reassign service destination search

Reassign Service no longer lists every customer in a dropdown. Staff search the destination by name, phone, email, customer account, or service account.

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Dialog | Search field, radio-style results, selected customer summary, confirmation checkbox, optional reason. Reassign stays off until a different live customer is selected and confirmed. |
| Search | Server-side, tenant-only, case-insensitive, 2+ characters, limit 20. Excludes the current owner, deleted, inactive, and recycled customers. |
| Save | Moves ownership only. Service number, package, credentials, expiry, status, RADIUS, and MikroTik stay. Invoices and payments stay on the current customer. No SMS or invoice. |
| Audit | `service.reassigned` with service account, previous and destination names, staff, and reason. |

Permissions: `services.reassign` (owner, admin, customer care, network engineer). Finance and technician cannot.

Proof: `reassign-format.test.ts`, `customer-lifecycle.test.ts`, `rbac.test.ts`.
