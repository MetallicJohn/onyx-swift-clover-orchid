# CR-041 Business tier credit

Business and enterprise packages may stay online after expiry until a configured **maximum outstanding** is reached. Credit is access-while-unpaid, not extra validity, and never unlimited.

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Packages | Tier (residential / business / enterprise), credit on/off, maximum KES, warning KES, disconnect at limit, continue after expiry, optional days cap. Max 0 is not configured — treated as residential. |
| Customer / service record | Inherit, enable, or disable. Service overrides customer, which overrides package. Extra services do not share another line's credit. |
| Billing | Residential still expires and does not stack unpaid invoices. Business below the limit stays Active (reason `business_credit`) and invoices stack per billing interval, including quarterly and yearly. |
| Credit limit | Outstanding ≥ maximum suspends (`credit_limit`), RADIUS/MikroTik disable, SMS once. Repeat cron runs do not duplicate. |
| Payment | Allocated to the selected service. Restores only that service when outstanding falls below the maximum. Duplicate callbacks are rejected. Failed SMS does not reverse the payment. |
| Portal | Shows tier, outstanding, limit, available credit, utilization, and a warning near the limit. Pay/STK cannot force restore. |
| Reports | Business services on credit, approaching/at limit, receivables, CSV. |
| Invoice PDF | Per-service previous balance, credit limit, outstanding, available credit. Dates follow the network format (default dd/mm/yy). |

Permissions: `billing.business_credit.view` (finance, customer care, owner, admin, support), `.manage` (finance, customer care, owner, admin), `.approve` / `.override` / `.restore` (finance, owner, admin), `.suspend` (finance, customer care, owner, admin). Technician cannot. RLS on `business_credit_events`.

Proof: `business-credit.test.ts`, `billing.test.ts`, `rbac.test.ts`. Live SMS/RADIUS NAS is not sent in CI.
